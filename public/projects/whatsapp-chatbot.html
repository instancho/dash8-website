      Battle scars · drag through the hard parts
      
Six production problems a demo never meets.

    

    

      
CH-01
Handwriting accuracy
Challenge
Single-pass parsing failed on ~30% of prescriptions — "Thyronorm" read as "Thyroxin", tablets parsed as injections, hallucinated extras.
Solution
Image preprocessing + the two-stage read-then-interpret pipeline, with patient corrections as the final safety net.

      
CH-02
WATI API quirks
Challenge
Underdocumented webhooks: outgoing messages fire webhooks too, button payloads land in odd fields, 20-char button limits, image URLs hidden in body.data.
Solution
A hardened webhook layer: owner-filtering, button-title reverse-mapping, 24-hour session handling.

      
CH-03
Webhook timeouts
Challenge
Parsing takes 15–30s across 4–8 API calls; WATI times out at ~10s, Render free tier adds 50s cold starts.
Solution
Instant 200 OK, a "Reading your prescription…" message, heavy work in a daemon thread, and a processing lock against duplicates.

      
CH-04
Name format chaos
Challenge
"Tab Pan 40" vs "PAN 40MG TABLET" — abbreviations, missing strengths, brand vs generic, Indian naming conventions.
Solution
The three-layer ladder at an 82% threshold, with form-filtering killing the most dangerous error: cross-form matches.

      
CH-05
Post-payment dead end
Challenge
After paying, patients hit a blank browser screen with no way back to the conversation.
Solution
The return URL hits the backend, which confirms payment and redirects to wa.me — straight back into the chat.

      
CH-06
Out-of-inventory medicines
Challenge
Not everything prescribed is among the 493 stocked items — but one unmatched medicine shouldn't block the whole order.
Solution
Two-tier pricing: matched items price instantly; the pharmacist prices the rest and a second payment link goes out automatically.

    Growth engine
    
A referral network that pays itself out.

    
Health workers and local shops refer patients with a code. Commissions are computed per order, tiers auto-adjust monthly to real performance, and payouts land directly in agents' UPI via cron — with daily WhatsApp summaries.

    

      
Bronze8%20 orders / mo

      
Silver11%40 orders / mo

      
Gold14%75 orders / mo

      
Platinum17%120 orders / mo

      
Diamond20%200 orders / mo

    

    
Beat 130% of target and you jump two tiers; fall under 60% and the target eases by 15% instead of churning you out. New agents are paid daily in month one to build trust.
